About

The group behind the work.

Blue Ivy Group is a construction, interiors and redevelopment firm working from Ghatkopar West, Mumbai. The firm takes on residential, commercial, redevelopment, turnkey and interior projects, and manages them from approvals through handover.

Recent and ongoing work spans Mumbai and beyond, including a commercial mall in Amravati, Pahuja Business Avenue in Sakinaka and Siddhanchal Residency in Ghatkopar.

The office is at 301, Excel Arcade, LBS Marg, Ghatkopar West, Mumbai 400 086, reachable on 022 2516 1818 and info@blueivygroup.in.
